## Later on 2026-11-12 (rel-citydetails2, rel-u21, rel-u21b, rel-ibn)

Probes in the workspace's `tools/plans/night-2026-11-10/probes/`, every other mod off.

- City Details names a moved percentage bonus: Amina holding Pachacuti's third bonus, the capital's Production reads
  "From Buildings 5", "Earth Shaker 0.5" with a "Food 10" child, and no "Other" (rel-citydetails2). Before the fix it
  read "Other 4.5": on 1.5.1 the income summary that City Details hands over carries no steps, so the names are now
  read from the full yield tree (`ui/game/lshuf-city-details-names.js` `incomeBase`). With Augustus's first bonus as
  well, "Other 4" stays: that's his per-town Production, which the game files under its own "Number of Towns" lines
  for Augustus himself too.
- Yi Sun-sin's first bonus on Amina: she received the free Fleet Commander (rel-u21). A coastal town with a Harbor
  read "No suitable location to spawn this Unit" for the Fleet Commander and for the game's own Galley alike
  (rel-u21b), so the town's spot decides it, not the deal.
- Trung Trac's first bonus on Amina: the Trung Nhi arrived with its commander promotion class (10 trees).
- Ibn Battuta's Wildcard bonus on Amina: she holds `TRAIT_LSHUF_IBN_WILDCARD`, and both of the story's requirement sets
  (`REQSET_ATTRIBUTE_79A_NARRATIVE`, `_REQUISITE`) point at it (rel-ibn). The 2 Wildcard points come from that
  narrative story; no player, Ibn himself included, bought an attribute node in 18 turns, so the reward wasn't seen.
  `tests/ibn-wildcard-story.mjs` covers the requirement offline.
