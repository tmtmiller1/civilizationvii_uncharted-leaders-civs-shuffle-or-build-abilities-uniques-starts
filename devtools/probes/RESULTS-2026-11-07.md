# Probe results, 2026-11-07

## Cross-age civilization bonus (crossage/)

Question: does a civilization bonus from another age work in an Antiquity game? It decides whether the civ build
screen's Every age option can let a player take other ages' ability bonuses.

Run: `probe pair crossage/lshuf-crossage-pair.json`, seed 4242, Antiquity, every other mod off. A is the plain game; B
adds America's Modern-age "+1 Production on Resources" (`TRAIT_MOD_FRONTIER_EXPANSION_PRODUCTION_ON_RESOURCES`) to
every Antiquity civilization's ability. Both games paint Cotton on three tiles next to the settler, found the city,
end a turn and turn on the yield icons. The player was Heian both times.

| Tile | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cotton tiles (3) | 1 to 1 Production | 1 to 2 Production |
| City center | 1 to 5 | 1 to 5 |
| Plain tiles (3) | unchanged | unchanged |

Verdict PASSED, watched: in B's capture the three Cotton tiles show two Production icons where A shows one
(`working_screenshots/2026-11-07-lshuf-crossage-side-by-side.png`). Bundles
`~/.tower-bench/probes/2026-11-07T22-19-22-lshuf-crossage-pair-A` and `...T22-20-13-lshuf-crossage-pair-B`.

The other ages' uniques (36 Exploration units, 30 Modern units, one Modern improvement) aren't in the Antiquity
database, and five Modern ability modifiers aren't either (France's Liberté government change, Joseon's two specialist
rules, Mexico's celebration tourism, Nepal's mountain tourism).

  traits don't include `TRAIT_GREECE`. `CityOperations.canStart(BUILD, BUILDING_PARTHENON)` was false: the

- The owner saw a "Civic Unlocked" popup with a blank icon at turn 11 of the first run (Heian's own civic, before
